# Liquidity Radar — Design Brief

Self-contained. Everything needed to design the product is in this one file,
including real data examples. Every number below was measured from the live
database on 2026-09-08 (601 deals, 1,469 source links, one month of output) and
is regenerated by `docs/stats.py` — none of it is estimated.

---

## The product

A deal-intelligence site for Indian markets. A pipeline watches financial news,
BSE/NSE filings and SEBI IPO papers around the clock and finds transactions
where **an individual is about to receive a large sum of money** — a promoter
selling a stake, a founder cashing out at IPO, a family shareholder exiting.

**The user is one kind of person: an investment banker prospecting those
individuals.** They scan for a name and a number, fast, several times a day,
often on a phone between meetings. Design for scanning speed and trust in the
number.

**References:** Mergermarket for the feed (dense, headline-first, professional).
PrivateCircle for the company profile (shareholding tables, promoter records).
This product is the hinge between them.

**Volume:** ~18 new deals/day. A feed you read to the bottom, not infinite scroll.

---

## Two hard problems

These are the brief. The layout is comparatively easy.

### 1. Most deals are mostly empty

| Field | Populated |
|---|---|
| company, deal type, summary, confidence | 100% |
| **amount** | **43%** |
| sources | 82% (18% have none) |
| seller | 27% |
| **named individual** | **14%** |
| exchange-confirmed | 1% |
| buyer | 16% (legacy rows only; 0% of new deals) |
| adviser / banker | 0% — not extracted yet |

The most common record in production is: a company name, a deal type, one
sentence, **no amount, no named individual, no buyer, no adviser**, and one
link. **Design that screen first** — it is the product. A mockup with every
field populated describes roughly 1 deal in 20.

Absence is information here. "Seller not yet named" is genuinely useful to a
banker, and should read as a deliberate statement rather than a gap where
something failed to load.

### 2. A number is not just a number

Every rupee figure arrived by one of four routes. Conflating them is the one
mistake that would damage the product's credibility.

| | Route | Render as | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fact** | The article stated it | `₹2,000cr` | 31% |
| **Derived** | stake % × market cap, both real numbers | `~₹706cr` · *15.26% × mkt cap* | 1% |
| **Estimate** | AI range estimate, unlisted company | `est. ₹2,000cr+` | 2% |
| **Unknown** | Nothing to go on | *Size undisclosed* | 57% |

This distinction must be legible **in the feed row while scanning at speed** —
not hidden in a tooltip or revealed only on click. A banker who quotes an AI
estimate to a client as a stated fact has been failed by the interface.

**Inventing a visual language for fact / derived / estimate / unknown that
survives being seen 25 times on one screen without becoming noise is the single
most valuable thing this design can contribute.**

## Screens

### A. Feed — primary, public

Dense scannable list, ~15–25 rows per desktop screen. Each row: confidence
indicator, **company name** (primary scan target), deal-type chip, amount +
provenance, one-line summary, named individuals when present (these are the
point of the product — when a name exists it should pull the eye), outlet +
relative time, source count when > 1.

**Filters** (sticky rail): date range, deal type, amount band, confidence,
listed/unlisted, outlet. Plus search.

**Sorting:** newest, largest, and *most covered* — 8 outlets on one deal means
the market is talking about it.

**Deal types** — twelve real values, by frequency: `DRHP filing` (161),
`strategic buyout` (104), `IPO-OFS` (85), `block deal` (78), `other` (38),
`promoter sale` (36), `open offer` (31), `PE secondary` (29), `PE primary` (26),
`unknown` (10), `bulk deal` (2), `IPO-DRHP` (1). Note `other` and `unknown` —
8% combined — must not look like a bug.

**Repetition:** the same company legitimately recurs as a story develops. One
₹2,000cr deal produced three separate rows within 90 minutes. The feed looks
broken unless the design answers this — grouping by company, a "3 related
updates" affordance, or a per-company timeline. Your call, but please solve it.

### B. Detail window — the centrepiece

A right-side drawer over the feed so context is never lost; deep-linkable.
Full-screen sheet under ~768px.

1. **Header** — company, deal type, confidence, exchange-verified badge, updated
2. **Quantum** — the number, large, with provenance treatment, and in smaller
   type the raw phrase behind it (`"15.26% × mcap ~Rs 4628cr"`) so the figure
   can be checked against its derivation
3. **Parties** — seller (who receives the money), individuals being paid, buyer
4. **Advisers** — sellside / buyside / legal. **Empty on every deal today.** If
   you conclude it shouldn't render when empty, that's a valid answer
5. **Coverage** — every article clustered into this deal: outlet, headline, link
6. **Company** — locked teaser for gated shareholding data

### C. States to draw explicitly

- Feed loading; feed empty; no results after filtering
- **The maximally sparse detail window** — no amount, no individual, no seller,
  no buyer, no adviser, one link. The most-viewed screen in the product
- **A company name up to 116 characters.** The field occasionally holds a whole
  headline. 22 rows exceed 40 chars. Since company name is the primary scan
  target, the truncation rule matters
- **A deal with zero sources** (18%) and **a deal with 39** (Zetwerk is real —
  Coverage needs collapse-after-N)

### D. Company profile — gated, design lightly

Promoter shareholding table: name, stake %, pledged %, QoQ change, contact, plus
that company's deals. This data doesn't exist in the pipeline yet, so
directional is fine — but **the locked teaser inside the public detail window is
the conversion surface** and should be designed properly.

## Constraint the design must work around

**77% of source links don't point at a publisher.** They are opaque Google News
redirect tokens; the real article URL is not recoverable. The *outlet name* is
reliable (it's parsed off the headline), the destination is not — those links
land on a Google interstitial. The UI should be honest about that rather than
promising a publisher page it can't deliver.

---

## Constraints

- **Light and dark**, both first-class
- **Dense data typography.** Tabular figures for every amount so columns align
  while scanning. Indian conventions — crore, `₹1,256cr`
- **Mobile is not secondary.** Read on a phone between meetings
- **Restrained.** A professional intelligence tool competing with terminal
  habits, not a consumer news app. Colour should carry meaning — confidence,
  provenance — and almost nothing else
- **Deliverable:** design tokens as CSS custom properties

**Out of scope:** auth screens, admin/CRM, email digests, contact management.

## Real data — six actual deals

Verbatim from the database. Note how little most of them carry.

**1. Rich — the exception (~1 in 20)**
```
company     Rentomojo            type  DRHP filing        confidence  high
amount      ₹1,256cr             from  "Rs 1,256 crore IPO size"      [stated]
individuals Nitish Mittersain
seller      promoters/selling shareholders
summary     Rentomojo IPO at ₹4,246 crore valuation; raises ₹1,256 crore.
sources     4
```

**2. Derived amount — show the working**
```
company     Foseco India         type  block deal         confidence  medium
amount      ~₹706cr              from  "15.26% x mcap ~Rs 4628cr = ~Rs 706cr"  [computed]
seller      Morgan Terrassen, Morganite Crucible, Alpha Wave India
individuals —
summary     Morgan Terrassen, Morganite Crucible sell 15.26% stake in Foseco India; Alpha Wave exits
sources     2
```

**3. Estimate only — never render as a point value**
```
company     BluPine Energy       type  strategic buyout   confidence  medium
amount      —                    band  OVER_2000          [band = AI estimate]
seller      Actis
summary     Actis-owned BluPine Energy 4GW renewable platform in major M&A auction.
sources     1
```

**4. Trap: raw text exists but is NOT an amount**
```
company     Biocon               type  block deal         confidence  medium
amount      —                    raw   "1.66 crore shares — no price stated"  [mcap_plausible]
seller      —   individuals —
summary     Biocon block deal of 1.66 crore shares; seller unnamed.
sources     1
```
`"1.66 crore shares"` is a *share count*, not money. Never render the raw phrase
as the amount.

**5. The common case — design this one first**
```
company     Kuku Technologies    type  DRHP filing        confidence  high
amount      ₹3,500cr             from  "₹3,500 crore IPO"  [stated]
seller      —   individuals —   buyer —   advisers —
summary     Kuku Technologies files confidential IPO papers for ₹3,500 crore.
sources     2
```

**6. Heavy coverage — 39 sources on one deal**
```
company     Zetwerk              type  IPO-OFS            amount  ₹2,600cr  [stated]
sources     39  (Economic Times, Moneycontrol, CNBC TV18, Business Standard, …)
```

**And the shape of the worst company value**, which the feed must truncate
gracefully — this is one row's `company` field, in full:
```
Rs 58,000 crore selloff by promoters, PE funds hits stock market. Why are they
cashing out now? - The Economic Times
```
